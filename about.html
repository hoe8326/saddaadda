THE SADDA STORY

Delving deep into Sadda Adda's roots, core ideologies, values, and why we are considered the absolute pinnacle youth getaway spot in Rewari!

A WAY OF CAFE LIFE

Where friendships begin & memories are logged.

Sadda Adda is miles more than a basic cafe. It is where friendships begin. Where late-night cravings meet unforgettable gaming conversations. Where every table holds pure laughter and wild stories. We set up in Rewari to create a space that feels like your own home's living room, but with spectacular neon visual grids, awesome food, and PS5 setups!

We believe that sharing a loaded cheese fry plaque or sipping rich Oreoshake blends should be an experience, not a simple meal. That is why our team meticulously calibrates the interior ambiance, volume tracks, board gaming stocks, and recipes to fit your style.

Our Mission

To continuously cultivate an accessible, luxurious dark neon-lit community getaway space in Haryana, offering gourmet comfort appetizers and fast, premium coffee brews at rates suited for youth budgets.

Our Vision

To scale Sadda Adda as the ultimate iconic national gaming café brand, recognized everywhere for youth pop culture integration, cozy seating patterns, lightning fast services, and memorable group laughs.

Our Core Values

Aesthetic integrity, exceptional food flavor consistency, absolute hospitality comfort, student friendliness, social bonding enablement, and fostering zero compromises on material quality.

OUR CAFE TIMELINE

2022

The First Adda Setup

Sadda Adda makes its official launch in Ganpat Nagar, Rewari! Armed with a small menu of 8 premium burgers, 3 delicious coffee variants, and an obsession to trigger great conversations.

2023

Gaming Ambiance Addition

We completely renovated Sadda Adda into an immersive gaming zone! Introduced glowing neon boards, customized console rooms, high-energy tracks and loaded toppings that fans loved.

2025

Visual Overhaul & App Launch

Achieved fully rated standing in Rewari with over 4.8 star customer ratings on Google directories! Expanded our menu of snacks, released digital takeaway panels, and upgraded backends.
